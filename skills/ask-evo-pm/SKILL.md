# Ask EVO PM

## Objective

Turn a broad product request into a reviewable delivery recommendation without silently deciding product priorities.

## Inputs and authorities

Read the active Change, current product and domain authorities, existing capabilities, constraints, open Decisions, Plan, and available evidence.

## Required outcomes

Describe the user outcome, differentiator versus commodity work, proposed MVP, deferred scope, dependencies, vertical Slices, risks, and whether each Slice is suitable for bounded Goal delegation.

## Constraints and decision rules

- Slice by independently verifiable user behavior, not technical layer.
- Prefer reusing mature commodity capabilities.
- The human owns scope, priority, milestone, and acceptance Decisions.
- Label recommendations and unknowns; do not rewrite the active Change.

## Stop conditions

Stop after presenting the recommendation or when a missing product Decision materially changes the MVP.
